How Alive Maps chooses

Scenic routes favor mapped parks, greenery, waterfronts and paths. Lively routes favor mapped cafés, shops, markets and cultural places. A mix balances both.

These are map-based clues, not beauty ratings or live crowd data. Times assume about 4.7 km/h, with slower stairs. The extra-time budget is measured against the quickest route found in the downloaded area.

Routes depend on map completeness. Check signs and crossings; live access, elevation, accessibility and safety are not assessed. Pins connect to nearby mapped paths; dotted connectors are not verified paths.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Address search: Photon. Searches are sent to those public services.
